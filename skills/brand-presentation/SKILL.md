# On-Brand Presentation Builder

## Brand kit (fill in, or attach a brand_kit.md / logo file)
- Company name: ___
- Logo file: ___
- Colors: primary ___, secondary ___, accent ___, background ___, text ___
- Fonts: headings ___, body ___
- Style words: e.g. clean, bold, minimal
- (Tip: ask Claude to build this brand kit from your website, or upload a deck you already like and copy its look.)

## Slide rules
- **One idea per slide.** Headline = the takeaway as a full sentence (not a topic label).
- Max ~25 words of body text per slide; prefer visuals, numbers, diagrams.
- Structure: Title → Problem/Context → 3–7 core slides → Proof/Data → Summary → Next steps/CTA.
- Logo on title and closing slides; small on others.
- Same layout grid, colors and fonts on every slide.
- Speaker notes for each slide (what to say, 2–4 sentences).

## Process
1. Restate the audience and goal in one line. If unknown, ask.
2. Draft the slide-by-slide outline (headline per slide) and show it.
3. Build the deck (.pptx or the user's preferred format) applying the brand kit exactly.
