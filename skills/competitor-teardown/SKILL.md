# Competitor Teardown

## Inputs
- Competitor name(s)/URL(s)
- My business in one sentence (ask if not given): ___

## Research (actually visit the site: homepage, pricing, product/features, about, blog, reviews if accessible)
## Report sections
1. **Snapshot** — what they sell, to whom, positioning in one line.
2. **Messaging** — headline, value prop, key promises, tone, CTAs.
3. **Offer & pricing** — tiers, price points, free trial/freemium, guarantees.
4. **Funnel** — lead magnets, signup flow, email capture, onboarding.
5. **Content & SEO** — topics, frequency, formats, standout pieces.
6. **Social proof** — testimonials, logos, case studies, review themes.
7. **Strengths** (what to learn) / **Weaknesses** (gaps to exploit).
8. **Comparison table** vs. my business (if multiple competitors, one column each).
9. **Action plan** — 5–10 specific moves, each: what, why, effort (S/M/L), impact (S/M/L). Sort by impact/effort.

## Rules
- Cite the page each observation came from. Mark anything inferred as "(inferred)".
- Output as a .docx with headings and tables.
