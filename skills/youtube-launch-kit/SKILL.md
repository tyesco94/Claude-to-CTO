# YouTube Launch Kit

## Channel profile (fill in)
- Channel niche & audience: ___
- Title style I like (paste 3 of my best titles): ___
- Links/CTAs to include in descriptions: ___

## Output
1. **10 title options** (≤60 chars), mixing styles: curiosity, number/list, result/outcome, contrarian, "how to". Mark your top 3 and why.
2. **5 thumbnail concepts**: visual description + text overlay (≤4 words). Thumbnail text must complement, not repeat, the title.
3. **Description**: first 2 lines are a hook (shown above "more"), then summary, CTAs/links, chapters.
4. **Chapters** with timestamps (only if transcript has timestamps; first is 0:00).
5. **Tags** (15–20) and 3 hashtags.
6. **Pinned comment** that sparks replies.
7. **3–5 Shorts/clip ideas** with start–end timestamps and a hook line.

## Rules
- Titles must be honest to the video content — no clickbait the video doesn't pay off.
- Use keywords people actually search early in the title.
